🧪 2. The Chemical Trap & Soil Degradation

As labor becomes expensive, farmers have turned to chemical herbicides. While quick, this reliance creates severe systemic issues:

  • Erosion of Soil Organicity: Chemical control displaces organic matter/biomass that traditional hoeing would return to the soil.
  • Health Hazards: Farmers often apply chemicals like 2,4-D without adequate respiratory or skin protection.
  • Non-Target Toxicity: Herbicides toxify non-target flora and reduce biodiversity among Ethiopia's broad-leaved species.
  • Disease Reservoirs: Dead biomass left by herbicides acts as a host for plant pathogens and insects.

🔍 3. The Flora Identification Challenge

A major hurdle for any weeding system is the immense complexity of Ethiopian weed flora:

  • Extreme Diversity: Over 88 major weed species from 23 families identified in maize fields alone (66 broad-leaved).
  • Morphological Variance: The presence of highly varied annual (71 species) and perennial (17 species) weeds requires adaptive removal strategies.
  • The 'Late Weeding Trap': Farmers intentionally delay weeding so biomass grows large enough for livestock feed, committing "yield suicide" by letting weeds steal nutrients during critical crop growth stages.

💧 4. Water Scarcity & The "Water Thieves"

Studies from the Worabe Agricultural Research Centre and SARI indicate that even with optimized irrigation scheduling (like a 9-day interval for potatoes), productivity is completely tied to field cleanliness. Weeds act as "water thieves," drastically reducing actual Water Productivity (WP).

🤖 Project Evolution: Version 2 (Current Generation)

The Version 2 Upgrade transforms the agile V1 platform into a fully autonomous, intelligent agricultural unit. This generation integrates complex mechanical actuation with a high-accuracy neural network to actively manage fields.

🦾 4-DOF Robotic Arm

Upgraded from simple movement, the rover now features a 4 Degrees-of-Freedom arm with a servo-actuated claw, allowing for surgical weed extraction without disturbing surrounding crop roots.

📦 Biomass Storage

Unlike V1, this iteration features onboard containment bins. Extracted weeds are stored locally, allowing the rover to transport biomass to composting or livestock feed stations automatically.

🧠 YOLOv8 High-Accuracy Neural Engine

The ESP32-CAM now feeds real-time video directly into a YOLOv8 detection model. With high-accuracy inference, the rover decisively categorizes plants as crops, grass, broadleaf, or fibrous weeds in milliseconds.
